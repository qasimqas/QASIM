Answers & Assurance

Frequently Inquired

Everything you need to know about our harvest cycles, high-fire ceramic durability, and ethical roastery practices.

All our beans are small-batch roasted within 48 hours of dispatch. Each bag is hand-stamped with the harvest origin, cupping notes, and exact roast date. We recommend allowing whole bean coffees to degas for 3–5 days post-roast for optimal clarity and nuance.
Yes. Our vessels are wheel-thrown or slip-cast using 100% non-toxic, lead-free high-fire stoneware fired to cone 10 (over 2,350°F). They are completely dishwasher safe and microwave durable. To preserve the tactile matte exterior glaze indefinitely, gentle hand-washing is occasionally encouraged.
Yes, our Curated Ritual subscription offers customized delivery frequencies of every 2, 3, or 4 weeks with an ongoing 15% discount on all coffee. You can rotate through single-origin microlots, swap roasts, pause during vacations, or cancel seamlessly anytime through your account portal.
We stand by our 30-day happiness guarantee. If your ceramic order arrives with flaws or doesn't match your expectations, we will replace or refund it immediately. For fresh roasted coffee, while we cannot accept returned food products, our concierge will issue a replacement roast if a flavor profile did not suit your pallete.
Yes, we ship to over 40 countries worldwide. Ceramic goods are packed using 100% biodegradable corrugated cardboard and cornstarch packing peanuts to ensure zero transit damage. All international logistics partner with certified carbon-neutral transport channels.
