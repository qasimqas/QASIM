## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, artisanal luxury tailored for discerning specialty coffee connoisseurs and handcrafted ceramics collectors. The brand ethos—"Brew Your Perfect Moment"—merges sensory warmth, ritualistic calm, and elevated minimalist commerce.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Warm Ritual & Comfort:** Tactile cream tones, rich espresso depths, and gentle caramel accents evoke the sensory experience of freshly pulled espresso in a hand-thrown ceramic mug.
- **Editorial Elegance:** Serene spatial cadence, high-contrast serif headlines, and deliberate framing reflect the restraint of high-end design magazines and boutique ateliers.
- **Quiet Modernity:** Balanced by functional, crisp modern typography and intuitive micro-interactions that feel polished, considerate, and seamless.

### Visual Style Direction
The aesthetic pairs **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Nuances**:
- Light, airy backgrounds grounded by deep espresso structure.
- Micro-borders rendered in warm stoneware tones instead of sterile greys.
- Muted, low-opacity coffee-tinted ambient shadows mimicking soft natural daylight across ceramic textures.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration directly from roast spectrums, steamed foam, and raw glazed stoneware. Contrast is tuned for accessibility while maintaining a soft, non-clinical glow.

### Color Roles & Application
- **Primary (`#3B2418` — Espresso Brown):** High-prominence headers, key primary interactive surfaces, dark navigation accents, and primary text glyphs.
- **Secondary (`#6F4E37` — Coffee Brown):** Secondary buttons, subheadings, muted iconography, and meta labels.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#C58B55` — Caramel):** Call-to-action highlights, rating stars, alert badges, stock status indicators, and focused states.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8F3EA` — Cream):** The ambient base layer for pages, viewports, and structural background surfaces.
- **Support Neutral Surface (`#FFFFFF` — Pure White):** Elevated product cards, interactive dropdowns, and flyout checkout sheets to ensure sharp contrast against the cream backdrop.
- **Border & Line Token (`#E8DCCB` — Warm Beige):** Delicate separators, input outlines, table divisions, and card perimeters.

## Typography

The pairing between `Playfair Display` and `Plus Jakarta Sans` creates a conversation between timeless heritage and modern functional performance.

- **Editorial Headings:** Display and headline levels utilize Playfair Display. Kerning is tightened slightly on large display formats to maintain compact editorial sophistication.
- **Product & System Body:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers pristine legibility at dense scales (e.g., origin profiles, tasting notes, dimensions, checkout tables).
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Small labels leverage uppercase treatment paired with widened tracking (`0.04em`–`0.08em`) to convey deliberate, catalog-like craftsmanship.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy favors editorial breathing room, generous framing, and architectural proportion over cramped density.

### Grid & Responsiveness
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers capped at max-width `1320px` to maintain comfortable readability.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Product showcases reflow from 3 or 4 columns down to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (&lt; 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Sticky commerce bars snap to the bottom edge with safe area integration.

### Spacing Cadence
- Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for compact internal card elements (e.g., rating star gaps, badge insets, metadata tags).
- Use `space-md` for standard form inputs, chip clusters, and list item spacing.
- Use `space-lg` and `space-xl` to segment primary narrative blocks, editorial imagery, and product card boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid stark drop-shadows that break the organic warmth of ceramic and coffee, elevation relies on warm-tinted ambient occlusion and tone layering.

### Shadow Characteristics
- **Tonal Color Cast:** Shadows strictly avoid pure black or neutral greys, using `rgba(59, 36, 24, 0.08)` (Espresso Brown at 8% opacity).
- **Subtle Resting State:** `0px 2px 8px rgba(59, 36, 24, 0.04)` paired with a `1px` border of `#E8DCCB`.
- **Raised / Hover State:** `0px 8px 24px rgba(59, 36, 24, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px rgba(59, 36, 24, 0.04)`.
- **Flyout / Modal Overlay:** `0px 16px 40px rgba(59, 36, 24, 0.12)`.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Base Layer:** Natural Cream (`#F8F3EA`).
2. **Surface Layer (Resting Cards, Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` bordered by `#E8DCCB`.
3. **Elevated Overlays:** Pure White modals or floating checkout sheets floating with ambient espresso-tinted diffusion.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary mirrors the gentle curvature of turned earthenware and softened rim edges.

- **Level 2 (Rounded):** Core UI controls, cards, and input fields utilize `0.5rem` (8px) base curvature.
- **Featured Surfaces & Modals:** Elevated containers utilize `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) or `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) for organic framing.
- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full`):** Reserved exclusively for dynamic badges, filter chips, rating capsules, and floating cart quantity indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Espresso CTA):** Solid `#3B2418` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius, `0.75rem 1.75rem` padding. On hover: shifts slightly to `#6F4E37` with subtle shadow elevation.
- **Accent Action (Caramel Highlight):** Solid `#C58B55` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, designed for "Add to Cart" or urgent promotions. On hover: brightness increases 5%.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #3B2418`, text `#3B2418`. On hover: fills with `#F8F3EA`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Base State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8DCCB`, text `#6F4E37`, pill shape (`rounded-full`), padding `0.375rem 1rem`.
- **Active State:** Background `#3B2418`, border `1px solid #3B2418`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Cards (Product & Editorial)
- **Product Tile:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8DCCB`, `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`). Image container features a subtle `#F8F3EA` background plate with `0.75rem` inner radius. Padding: `1rem` on container.
- **Editorial Callout:** Background `#F8F3EA`, border `1px solid #E8DCCB`, featuring Playfair Display typography and caramel accent links.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8DCCB`, `0.5rem` radius, typography `body-md` in `#3B2418`.
- **Focus State:** Border color shifts to `#C58B55` with an ambient glow ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 139, 85, 0.2)`. No harsh browser outlines.

### Badges & Ratings
- **Collection Badges ("Limited Harvest", "Single Origin"):** Pill-shaped, background `#F8F3EA`, border `1px solid #E8DCCB`, text `#6F4E37`, typography `label-sm`.
- **Star Rating:** Minimalist 5-star cluster rendered in `#C58B55`, paired with numerical reviews in `body-sm` (`#6F4E37`).

### Mini-Cart & Status Indicators
- **Cart Badge:** Compact pill or circle in `#C58B55` with bold white numerals (`#FFFFFF`), anchored to the espresso navigation icon.
- **Roast Profile Meter:** Segmented visual bar with unfilled segments in `#E8DCCB` and filled roast levels in `#3B2418`.
