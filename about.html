A Letter From The Founders

"We built Brew & Bean to reclaim the quiet luxury of the ordinary morning."

In our former corporate lives, coffee was an afterthought—a scalding liquid gulped down from a disposable paper cup in transit to a screen. It was transactional caffeine, devoid of warmth or delight.

In 2021, we brought a vintage 15kg cast-iron roaster and two Shimpo pottery wheels into a former timber warehouse in Southeast Portland. We wanted to reunite the bean with the vessel. When you hold a heavy ceramic cup crafted from mineral-rich clay, warm with freshly roasted coffee that paid a smallholder family a prosperous income, you aren't simply awake—you are present.

We hope our coffee offers you that same quiet grace every dawn.

Julian Sterling & Clara Sterling Founders, Brew & Bean Atelier
